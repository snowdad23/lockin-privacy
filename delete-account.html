LockIn — Delete your account

Last updated 1 September 2026

LockIn is developed by an individual developer (contact: lockinpill@gmail.com). This page explains how to request deletion of your LockIn account and the data associated with it.

How to delete your account

In the app: open Profile → Account → Delete account, confirm, and re-enter your password. This is instant and requires no waiting period.

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request deletion by email instead:

  1. Email lockinpill@gmail.com from the address you signed up with.
  2. Include the subject line "Delete my LockIn account".
  3. We will verify the request and delete your account within 30 days, and confirm by email once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email and authentication credentials)
  • Your community posts and comments
  • Your blocks and hidden-posts list
  • Any photos you attached to posts
  • Your push notification token

What is retained, and why

DataRetention
Reports you have filed against other content Kept indefinitely, with your identity removed, so there is a moderation record that the report was reviewed. This does not identify you after deletion.
On-device data (habits, focus sessions, XP, achievements, settings) This never leaves your device and is not part of the account deletion request. It is removed by uninstalling the app.

Deleting your account does not delete the LockIn app or browser extension from your device — see the Privacy Policy for on-device data removal.
